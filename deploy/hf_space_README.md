# India maternal-risk discordance explorer

Explainable decision support that classifies Indian districts into Priority / High-Risk, Coverage Gap,
Best Performer and Low Burden tiers from NFHS-5 and HMIS data, with TreeSHAP district diagnostics,
scheme mapping (LaQshya, PMSMA, JSY, POSHAN Abhiyaan) and a what-if simulator.

Research prototype: outputs are associative and must be validated with MDSR, CRS/verbal-autopsy and
facility audits before any resource-allocation decision.
